Food and campfire

Simple, hearty and planned for your group.

Food at Galimale Homestay is home cooked and can include South Indian, Malnad-style, vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes. Please confirm your meal preference, guest count and dates before arrival.

  • Day-one check-in from 2:00 p.m.
  • Checkout at 11:00 a.m.
  • Lunch, evening coffee, dinner, morning coffee and breakfast plans
  • Campfire with music allowed till 10:00 p.m. at moderate volume
